# Work Image Report Sample Design

## Goal

Create a static two-page PDF sample for the `工作／見客` branch of the A2O
post-consultation report. It is a presentation example only: brand names,
products, prices, and programme pricing are intentionally replaceable before
client delivery.

## Future Report Routing

The final report is selected from answer two of the assessment:

| Q2 answer index | Report branch |
| --- | --- |
| 1-2 | 工作／見客 |
| 3-4 | 約會／認識對象 |
| 5-6 | 未能明確分類的場合 |

The first two pages vary by branch. The shared guide will later append only
pages 3 through 14 from `a2o_menswear_guide.pdf`; its original cover, concept,
and all later pages are excluded.

## Sample PDF Scope

The immediate deliverable has two A4 pages only and does not yet merge the
shared guide or connect to the website.

### Visual Direction

- Approved direction: dark editorial A2O look.
- Base: deep charcoal and warm grey.
- Accent: restrained A2O pink.
- Typography: large serif Chinese headline with clean sans-serif details.
- Purpose: reads as a personal, credible image-consultant report on a phone or
  as a WhatsApp attachment.

### Page 1 - Work Image Direction

Headline: `你的工作形象穿搭方向`

Opening point: professional dress should establish trust before the client
hears the pitch.

It contains three illustrative outfits with an `示範品牌／示範預算` label so no
example is mistaken for a current product recommendation:

1. Client meeting: navy knit polo, cream straight trousers, black loafers.
2. Everyday professional: charcoal overshirt, white T-shirt, dark grey straight trousers.
3. Formal meeting: lightweight dark blazer, pale-blue shirt, dark tailored trousers.

Each outfit has a short use case, styling point, and an illustrative Hong Kong
dollar budget.

### Page 2 - A2O Programme Example

Headline: `A2O 男士形象提升計劃`

Illustrative price: `HK$5,980`.

Illustrative inclusions:

- personal proportion analysis and image positioning
- three practical outfit directions
- hair and grooming direction
- shopping list and wardrobe priorities
- WhatsApp follow-up

The page closes with the existing A2O WhatsApp consultation CTA. The price and
service list are sample content and must be replaced with approved commercial
terms before sending a client report.

## Acceptance Checks

- Exactly two pages.
- Traditional Chinese is legible with no clipped lines.
- Both pages use the approved dark editorial direction.
- Every brand/price example is visibly marked as a sample.
- No real client data is present.
